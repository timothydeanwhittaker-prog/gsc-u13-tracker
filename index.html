Warm Down
Tap to work through stretches
⌄
○
Quadriceps Stretch
Standing, hold one foot behind you and pull it gently toward your glutes. Keep knees together and push hips forward. 20 sec each leg.
○
Hamstring Stretch
Kneel on one knee, extend the other leg straight in front with heel on the ground. Hinge forward at the hips, back straight. 20 sec each leg.
○
Calf Stretch
One foot forward bent slightly, back leg straight with heel flat on the ground. Feel the stretch in the back of the lower leg. 20 sec each side.
○
Butterfly Groin Stretch
Sit with soles of feet together. Let knees drop toward the floor and use your elbows to gently push down on your knees. Hold 20–30 sec.
○
Pigeon Pose (Glutes)
One leg underneath bent at the knee, shin parallel to the front. Other leg straight behind. Feel it in the hip and glute. 20 sec each side.
○
Back Twists
Lie on your back, bring one knee across your body to the opposite side. Keep both shoulders flat on the grass. Hold 20 sec each side.
